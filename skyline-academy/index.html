About Skyline Academy

Dedicated online academic support and EdTech guidance for open university students.

Our Mission & Purpose

Skyline Academy was founded to bridge the learning gap for students pursuing distance and open university education. Distance learning offers great flexibility, but students often face challenges in grasping complex syllabi, structuring assignments, and preparing systematically for examinations.

We offer mentorship, structured online classes, and curated study notes to ensure every learner receives the highest quality academic assistance.

University Support Scope

The academy currently supports students enrolled in various undergraduate programmes at Sri Narayana Guru Open University (SGOU), including B.Com, BBA, B.A English, B.A Malayalam, B.A Sociology, and B.A History.

Skyline Academy is an independent academic support service. We are not part of SGOU and do not issue degrees or evaluate official submissions.
